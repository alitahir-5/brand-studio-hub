How to use this hub

Writing

  • Open Write, pick what you need (social post, LinkedIn, ad, email, web section, script), add the facts, and press Write.
  • The writer follows this brand's voice and rules. Give it real facts; it won't invent prices or numbers.
  • Copy the result and adjust before posting. Ask for 2 or 3 versions to compare.

Videos and visuals

  • Press Make a video in this look to open the Video Studio with brand colours, type and logo set.
  • Use Write with AI in the studio to draft scenes, then edit and export as a video file.
  • Logos, screenshots and brand graphics are in Logos and assets, each with a download button.

Rules to keep

  • Blue for Pro and Fleet, cyan for Home and Lite, gradient for the whole platform.
  • Customer-facing copy ships in English and Bangla, with ৳ for prices.
  • Sentence case, "your fleet", facts over hype. No emoji, no em dashes.
  • LocomotePro is one word with a capital P. BRTA in capitals. Mandate date in full: 1 August 2026.
