# How to use this design system

A short guide for the marketing team. You do not need to be a designer. Everything Surfaced Social looks and sounds like is saved here, so Claude (and you) can make on-brand work quickly.

## What is in here, and where

- **Overview (brand book):** the rules for voice, colour, type, logo and layout. Read this once.
- **Colors:** every brand colour with its hex value and where to use it.
- **Typography:** the two fonts (Anton for headlines, Inter for everything else) and their sizes.
- **Spacing:** spacing steps, corner radii and the one card shadow.
- **Assets:**
  - Logos: the official logo files.
  - Icons: the Google Play glyph.
  - App screenshots: real screens from the app, raw and in phone frames.
  - App Store: the five iPad store screenshots.
  - Social posts: 26 finished 1080 x 1080 posts (launch series, content posts, feature posts) to copy the style from.
  - Covers: the Facebook page cover.
- **Voice rules:** in the brand book under "Content fundamentals", with real lines you can reuse.

## How to ask Claude to make something

Name the design system in your request so Claude loads these rules. Be specific about size, topic and channel. Examples:

- "Make a 1080x1080 Instagram post about bookmarks using the Surfaced Social Design System."
- "Make an App Store screenshot set using the Surfaced Social Design System."
- "Write 5 launch captions in Surfaced voice."
- "Make a LinkedIn banner (1584x396) using the Surfaced Social Design System, dark ink background."
- "Turn this press release into 3 X posts in Surfaced voice."
- "Check this caption against the Surfaced Social brand rules and fix anything off-brand."

Tips:
- Say which background you want: dark (ink), blue or white.
- Say which screenshot to use if the post shows the app (for example "use phone-home").
- Paste any copy you already have; Claude will keep your words and fix the style.

## Colour rules

- Two brand blues plus neutrals. That is the whole palette.
  - Brand blue `#0088db` (blue-600): main colour, blue backgrounds, highlighted headline words.
  - Accent cyan `#00ade2` (cyan-500): highlights on dark backgrounds, the wordmark colour.
  - Ink `#0e1a24` (ink-900): headings and the dark background.
  - White `#ffffff`: the default background.
  - Soft cyan `#f0fafd` (cyan-50): tinted cards and alternating sections.
- Use the dark ink band for the big statement (the manifesto) or the final call to action. One per page or set.
- Never put cyan text on white: it is too faint to read.
- Do not add new colours, gradients or textures.

## Type rules

- Headlines: Anton. Everything else: Inter.
- Sentence case always ("Your feed. Your choice."), never Title Case, never ALL CAPS.
- Highlight one or two headline words in brand blue (on white) or cyan (on dark). No more.
- Keep body text 16px or larger on screens.

## Logo do's and don'ts

Do:
- Use `logo.png`. It is the official file.
- Place it on white, or inside a white rounded tile on a blue background.
- Give it room: at least the height of the "S" stroke on every side.

Don't:
- Don't use `logo.svg`: it shows up black. Ask for a fixed SVG if a tool needs vector.
- Don't recolour, stretch, rotate, outline or add shadows to it.
- Don't place it straight on busy photos or screenshots.

## House rules (from the brand book)

- No emoji. Anywhere.
- Use contrast framing: "surfaced by people, not algorithms", "works for you, not against you".
- Be honest about iOS: it is "coming soon". Never say "Available on the App Store" or link to it.
- The official contact is info@surfacedsocial.com.
- The official name is "Surfaced Social". "Surfaced" is fine after first mention and in headlines.
- Handle `@surfacedsocial`, site surfacedsocial.com.

## How to download things

- **A logo, screenshot or post:** open the Assets view, click the file, then use its download button. Each file downloads at full size.
- **A finished design Claude made for you:** ask Claude to "export it as a PNG" (or PDF). Claude gives you a file to download.
- **Colour values:** copy them from the Colors view.

## How to request changes

- **Small fixes** (a colour value, a usage note, a typo): ask Claude in chat, for example "In the Surfaced Social Design System, change the caption size to 14px."
- **New assets** (a new logo file, new screenshots): send the files to Claude and say which group they belong in.
- **Comments:** leave a comment on any part of this page to flag something for the team.
- Anything that changes the brand itself (new colours, a new logo) should be agreed with the brand owner first.
